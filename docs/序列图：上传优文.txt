上传序列图

```mermaid
sequenceDiagram
    actor User as 用户
    participant UploadPage as 上传界面
    participant Camera as 摄像头/图片上传
    participant OCRService as 文字识别服务
    participant ThemeService as 主题识别服务
    participant ParagraphService as ParagraphService
    participant ParagraphStore as 语段数据存储
    participant Corpus as 语料库
    participant ParagraphDetailPage as 语段展示界面
    participant CollectionService as CollectionService

    User->>UploadPage: 选择拍照上传或图片上传
    UploadPage->>Camera: captureOrSelectImage()
    Camera-->>UploadPage: 返回图片
    UploadPage->>OCRService: recognizeText(image)
    OCRService-->>UploadPage: 返回识别出的文字
    UploadPage-->>User: 将识别文字填入语段内容
    User->>UploadPage: 填写内容、主题、标签
    UploadPage->>ThemeService: detectTheme(content)
    ThemeService-->>UploadPage: 返回推荐作文主题
    UploadPage-->>User: 将推荐主题设为作文主题默认值
    User->>UploadPage: 点击上传
    UploadPage->>ParagraphService: createParagraph(paragraphData)
    ParagraphService->>ParagraphStore: save(paragraph)
    ParagraphStore-->>ParagraphService: 返回保存后的语段信息
    ParagraphService->>Corpus: refresh()
    ParagraphService-->>UploadPage: 返回上传结果和语段ID
    UploadPage->>ParagraphDetailPage: goToParagraphDetail(paragraphId)
    ParagraphDetailPage->>ParagraphService: getParagraphById(paragraphId)
    ParagraphService->>ParagraphStore: findById(paragraphId)
    ParagraphStore-->>ParagraphService: 返回语段详情
    ParagraphService-->>ParagraphDetailPage: 返回语段详情
    ParagraphDetailPage->>CollectionService: getCollectionCount(paragraphId)
    CollectionService-->>ParagraphDetailPage: 返回收藏数
    ParagraphDetailPage-->>User: 展示上传成功后的语段详情和收藏数
```

上传规则表

U1	用户未登录	跳转到登录界面
U2	内容为空	提示“请输入语段内容”
U3	未选择主题	提示“请选择作文主题”
U4	内容过短	提示“语段内容过短”
U5	表单合法	保存语段
U6	保存成功	显示“上传成功”并进入对应语段展示界面
U7	用户选择拍照或图片上传	调用文字识别服务并将识别结果填入内容输入框
U8	文字识别失败	提示“文字识别失败，请手动输入或重新上传”
U9	语段内容填写完毕	自动判断语段主题并设为作文主题默认值
U10	主题识别失败	保留作文主题为空或原选择值，允许用户手动选择
U11	用户选择标签	“应用文”“读后续写”“名言”“动作”“心理活动”与“开头”“结尾”等标签处于同一层级，可作为普通标签选择
