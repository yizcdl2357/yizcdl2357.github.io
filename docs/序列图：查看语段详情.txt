```mermaid
sequenceDiagram
    actor User as 用户
    participant CorpusPage as 语料库界面
    participant ParagraphDetailPage as 语段展示界面
    participant ParagraphService as ParagraphService
    participant ParagraphStore as 语段数据存储
    participant AuthService as AuthService
    participant CollectionService as CollectionService

    User->>CorpusPage: 点击某个语段
    CorpusPage->>ParagraphDetailPage: openParagraph(paragraphId)
    ParagraphDetailPage->>ParagraphService: getParagraphById(paragraphId)
    ParagraphService->>ParagraphStore: findById(paragraphId)
    ParagraphStore-->>ParagraphService: 返回语段详情
    ParagraphService-->>ParagraphDetailPage: 返回语段详情
    ParagraphDetailPage->>CollectionService: getCollectionCount(paragraphId)
    CollectionService-->>ParagraphDetailPage: 返回收藏数
    ParagraphDetailPage->>AuthService: getCurrentUser()
    AuthService-->>ParagraphDetailPage: 返回当前用户或未登录状态
    ParagraphDetailPage->>CollectionService: isCollected(userId, paragraphId)
    CollectionService-->>ParagraphDetailPage: 返回收藏状态
    ParagraphDetailPage-->>User: 展示语段详情、收藏数和收藏状态

    User->>ParagraphDetailPage: 点击收藏或取消收藏
    ParagraphDetailPage->>CollectionService: toggleCollection(userId, paragraphId)
    CollectionService-->>ParagraphDetailPage: 返回新的收藏状态和收藏数
    ParagraphDetailPage-->>User: 更新收藏状态和收藏数
```

语段详情规则表
规则编号	条件	系统行为
D1	语段存在	展示内容、主题、标签和收藏数
D2	语段不存在	显示语段不存在提示
D3	用户未登录时点击收藏	跳转到登录界面
D4	用户未收藏该语段时点击收藏	创建收藏记录并增加收藏数
D5	用户已收藏该语段时点击取消收藏	删除收藏记录并减少收藏数
