```mermaid
sequenceDiagram
    actor User as 用户
    participant CorpusPage as 语料库界面
    participant SearchService as SearchService
    participant ParagraphStore as 语段数据存储
    participant CollectionService as CollectionService

    User->>CorpusPage: 进入语料库界面
    CorpusPage->>SearchService: getParagraphsOrderByCollectionCount()
    SearchService->>ParagraphStore: findAll()
    ParagraphStore-->>SearchService: 返回语段列表
    SearchService->>CollectionService: attachCollectionCounts(paragraphs)
    CollectionService-->>SearchService: 返回带收藏数的语段列表
    SearchService-->>CorpusPage: 返回按收藏数排序的语段列表
    CorpusPage->>CorpusPage: formatParagraphPreview(maxLength=300)
    CorpusPage-->>User: 展示正文预览、主题、标签和收藏数
```

语料库展示规则表
规则编号	条件	系统行为
B1	展示语料库列表	每篇语段不显示标题
B2	正文字符数大于300	仅显示前300字并追加省略号
B3	正文字符数不超过300	显示完整正文
